---
name: evaluate-idea
description: "Evaluate an idea for a YouTube video, Short, or music track and recommend whether to proceed, revise it, or research further."
---

Read the [channel identity](../../../canal/00-config/channel-identity.md) and the user's idea. Use the [idea template](../../../canal/ideas/idea-template.md) if it needs to be recorded. Consult the [published index](../../../canal/published-index.md) when it helps identify topics the channel has already covered.

1. State each viable angle's promise from the viewer's perspective. Compare its fit with the channel, clarity, what can be shown or verified, and the work it would take to produce.
2. Identify the main objection and the uncertainty that could change your recommendation. Name the missing information and a small test that could resolve it. If the channel identity is blank, use what the user has provided and list the open decisions.
3. Recommend whether to proceed, revise the idea, or research further. Give the preferred angle, your reason, and the next step. When asked to move into production, carry the chosen promise into the production brief.
