Apply develop-video to the assigned stage. Read the brief and use the research, script, or visual plan according to the production's progress. Work with the available material and flag decisions that depend on missing information.

Check that the opening makes clear what the viewer will gain and that the body delivers on the promise. Follow the agreed structure. If it is open, propose continuous narration or divisions that suit the material. Flag claims that need support, places where the thread becomes hard to follow, and transitions worth changing. For Remotion, tie scenes to narration, name the evidence to show, and distinguish estimated durations from timings verified against the narration.

Send the coordinating assistant your proposed changes, where they apply, their priority, and the next concrete step.
