---
name: prepare-publishing
description: "Prepare a YouTube title, thumbnail or cover concept, description, and chapters for a video, Short, or music track."
---

Open the production brief and `publishing.md`. Read the [channel identity](../../../canal/00-config/channel-identity.md) and, when a thumbnail is needed, the [thumbnail guide](../../../canal/00-config/thumbnail-guide.md). Use the script, plan, or available final material as appropriate. If the brief tracks edit versions, use the selected cut to verify the promise.

1. Propose a title and thumbnail or cover concept that together reflect what the production delivers. When offering alternatives, recommend one and explain how it fits the promise.
2. Draft the description and include relevant sources and credits. Include chapters when they fit the format and their timestamps can be checked against the final cut. Mark them as pending when the final timestamps are unavailable.
3. When asked to update files, save the proposal in `publishing.md`. Once publication is confirmed, record the final URL there, update the brief, and link it from the [published index](../../../canal/published-index.md).
